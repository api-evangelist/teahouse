Introduction Mean reversion is a well-established trading strategy that seeks to capitalize on temporary price deviations from an expected value. While the concept is widely known, Teahouse’s new strategy — RevertX — applies a specialized pairs trading approach to $BTC and $ETH, leveraging statistical models to identify profitable trading opportunities. TL;DR: RevertX capitalizes on mean reversion between $ETH and $BTC by tracking price deviations and trading when they exceed a threshold.
